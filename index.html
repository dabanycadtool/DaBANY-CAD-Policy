Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: August 30, 2026

DaB ANY CAD Tools ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our software plugins handle data when used within Autodesk AutoCAD or Civil 3D.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your design privacy. We do not collect, view, store, or transmit any of your CAD drawing files (.dwg), project data, intellectual property, or personal documents.

To manage software licensing and ensure authorized use, our plugins process minimal technical data:

  • Hardware Fingerprint (Machine ID): A secure, hashed identifier derived from your computer's processor is used exclusively to lock and validate your license key. This code cannot track your personal identity or access private files.
  • License Keys: Purchased or trial license keys entered into the application are validated and stored locally on your device.

2. How We Store Your Data

Your active license key is stored securely on your local machine within the standard Windows Registry (HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\DAB_ANY\...). We do not operate remote user tracking accounts or cloud databases tracking your workflow activity.

3. Third-Party Services

Our software does not use third-party analytics trackers, ad networks, or data brokers. Network communication is strictly limited to manual license activation or visiting our support pages.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised effective date.
